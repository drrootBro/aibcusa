About AIBC International LLC

AIBC International LLC is an independent software company based in New York, United States.

We develop practical AI-powered software focused on productivity, knowledge management, learning, and content creation. Our mission is to make advanced AI technology accessible through simple, privacy-first, and user-controlled software.

We prioritize lightweight tools, clear user workflows, lifetime licensing options where appropriate, and Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) AI integrations. Software products are developed under the RootBro Software brand.
