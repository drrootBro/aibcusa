Spanish Phrases for a Doctor Visit · Frases en español para la consulta

This page is for the person asking the questions: front-desk staff, nurses, and bilingual family members who need the basic flow of a visit in Spanish. Use “usted” (formal) by default.

Esta página es para quien hace las preguntas en la consulta: recepción, enfermería y familiares bilingües. Usa «usted» por respeto.

Pronunciation tips · Cómo pronunciarlo

  • Use “usted,” not “tú,” with adults, especially older patients. It sounds like “oos-TED.”
  • Double r rolls. “Receta” has a single tap, but “perro” rolls. For medical words, a clear single tap is understood.
  • H is silent. “Hola” is OH-lah, and “hoy” is oy.

Common mistakes · Errores comunes

  • Using “tú” with an older patient. It can sound disrespectful. Start with “usted.” · Empieza con «usted».
  • Asking “¿Entiende?” as the only check. Most people answer “sí” out of politeness. Use teach-back instead. · Pide que lo repitan.
  • Mixing English words into long Spanish sentences. Short, complete sentences are clearer than a blend of both. · Frases cortas y completas.

Before you rely on a phrase list · Antes de usar frases

A reliable habit is teach-back: after explaining, ask the patient to say it back. A nod does not prove understanding, so confirm with an open question.

No basta con que el paciente asienta: pídele que lo repita con sus propias palabras.

FAQ · Preguntas frecuentes

Should I say “usted” or “tú” to patients? / ¿Debo usar «usted» o «tú»?

Use “usted” with adults unless they invite you to use “tú.” Younger patients and children are often addressed with “tú.”

Usa «usted» con adultos. Con niños y jóvenes se usa «tú».

Is a phrase list enough for a full visit? / ¿Basta una lista de frases para toda la consulta?

No. Phrases help with greetings, forms, and simple instructions. For diagnoses, consent, and complex questions, use a qualified medical interpreter.

No. Para diagnósticos y consentimientos se necesita un intérprete médico calificado.

What if the patient answers faster than I understand? / ¿Y si el paciente responde más rápido de lo que entiendo?

Say “Más despacio, por favor” (mahs deh-SPAH-syo, por fah-VOR), then repeat what you understood and ask “¿Es correcto?”

Pide «más despacio, por favor» y repite lo que entendiste para confirmar.
