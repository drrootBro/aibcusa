Explaining a Procedure in Spanish · Explicar un procedimiento

These phrases help you prepare a patient for a procedure: fasting, what to expect, and who will drive them home. Informed consent itself needs a qualified medical interpreter.

Estas frases sirven para preparar al paciente. El consentimiento informado requiere un intérprete médico calificado.

Pronunciation tips · Cómo pronunciarlo

  • “Procedimiento” is long but regular: proh-seh-dee-MYEN-to. Break it into syllables and say it slowly.
  • Gender agreement: “dormido” for a man, “dormida” for a woman. Use slashes if unsure.
  • “Ayunas” means fasting: ah-YOO-nahs. Patients know the phrase “estar en ayunas.”

Common mistakes · Errores comunes

  • Using a phrase list as consent. Consent needs a qualified interpreter, not a script. · Usa intérprete calificado.
  • Using a family member as interpreter. It can compromise accuracy and the patient’s privacy. · Evita familiares como intérpretes.
  • Skipping the questions step. Always ask “¿Qué preguntas tiene?” and wait. · Espera las preguntas.

Before you rely on a phrase list · Antes de usar frases

Do not use a phrase list or a family member to obtain consent. Federal rules and hospital policies call for a qualified interpreter. Use these lines to reinforce instructions the interpreter has already explained.

No uses estas frases para obtener el consentimiento; sirven para reforzar lo que el intérprete ya explicó.

FAQ · Preguntas frecuentes

Can a family member interpret consent? / ¿Puede un familiar interpretar el consentimiento?

In most hospitals no. A qualified interpreter should be used because accuracy and neutrality matter. Family can support but should not replace the interpreter.

Normalmente no; el hospital debe ofrecer un intérprete calificado.

How do I say “fasting” for a blood test? / ¿Cómo se dice «en ayunas» en una prueba de sangre?

“Tiene que estar en ayunas por ocho horas.” Water is usually allowed; confirm with the lab.

Fasting means no food or drink except water, usually for eight hours.

What is the safest way to confirm understanding? / ¿Cuál es la forma más segura de confirmar?

Use teach-back: “Para estar seguro, ¿me puede explicar lo que va a pasar?” Then correct any gaps.

Ask the patient to explain the plan in their own words.
