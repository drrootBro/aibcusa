Japan Must Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · 日本語

Japan Must is an offline English–Japanese translator for travelers. It translates typed text, photos of signs and menus, and spoken conversation, and it explains Japanese dishes and flags common allergens. This policy explains how Japan Must handles information.

In short: Japan Must works without an internet connection. Your text, photos, and voice are processed on your device. AIBC International LLC does not require an account and does not collect, receive, store, or sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

None. Japan Must does not ask you to create an account, and it does not send your translations, photos, voice recordings, or location to AIBC International LLC or to any third party.

Camera And Photos

Japan Must requests camera access (and, if you choose a saved picture, photo library access) so you can scan Japanese text such as signs and menus. Images are processed on your device to recognize and translate text. They are not uploaded.

Microphone And Speech

Japan Must requests microphone access for voice-to-voice translation. Your speech is recognized and translated on your device, and the Japanese reply is spoken by on-device text-to-speech. Recordings are not uploaded.

Text You Type

Text you enter is translated on your device and is not sent anywhere. Anything the app keeps on your device stays there and is removed when you delete the app.

Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

Japan Must contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. AIBC International LLC does not receive your payment card details. Apple and Google handle purchase information according to their own privacy policies.

Menu And Allergen Information

Dish explanations and allergen flags are provided for reference only and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Recipes vary by restaurant. If you have a food allergy, always confirm ingredients with the restaurant staff.

Children

Japan Must is a general travel tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke camera, photo, or microphone access in your device settings. Scanning or voice translation will stop working; the other features keep working.
  • You can delete the app to remove all data it stored on your device.

Changes To This Policy

If we change how Japan Must handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact admin@aibcusa.com.
