MedEar Privacy Notice MedEar is a medical visit assistant for patients who need help understanding English medical conversations. Microphone and Speech Data: MedEar requests microphone access only to record and transcribe speech during a visit. Audio recordings and transcripts for Listen & Decode mode are processed on device where supported and stored locally on the user's device. AIBC International LLC does not receive, store, or sell users' audio recordings or medical visit transcripts. Local Storage: Visit history, transcripts, detected medical terms, and generated visit summaries are stored locally on the device. Sensitive visit text is protected using local encrypted storage where supported. Translation: Express Symptoms mode may open Google Translate to translate user-entered or spoken symptom descriptions from Spanish to English. Text submitted to Google Translate is processed according to Google's privacy policy. Health Disclaimer: MedEar is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Content generated by MedEar is for personal reference and communication support only. Users should always consult a qualified healthcare professional. Data Sharing: AIBC International LLC does not sell personal information. We do not use MedEar visit content for advertising. Contact: For privacy questions, contact admin@aibcusa.com.
